| OQ-14 | Does C-04's floor of five companies apply to the size of a pool shown to the pool's own members and to the operator? If the floor does not apply to members, an owner could be told "three other companies want this", which makes pooling easier to explain and gives away more. | Sponsor | Whether pools below five are described to owners; what the owner page says |
| OQ-15 | After an owner closes an engagement, may the same requirement be given another provider? Allowing a second engagement means deciding what happens to the first one's recorded payments and to the pool the requirement sits in. | Sponsor | Whether an owner can recover from choosing the wrong provider; the shape of `engagements` |
