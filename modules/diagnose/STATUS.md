# Diagnose module: status

Owner: the diagnose module. Paths: `modules/diagnose/`, `app/diagnose/`, `app/api/companies/[id]/assessments/`, and migrations 0005, 0006 and 0007 in `drizzle/`. Last updated 5 October 2026 (the page rebuilt, slice 10a).

This is slice 3, Diagnose v0: from the owner's confirmed description of the factory, a ranked list of things to do about the problems they named, each labelled AI or not, with a benefit range, cost (one-time, every year, hidden), what to do first and the risk. The owner decides on each idea and signs the assessment off. Protected paths touched: data scope (a company's assessments, read and written only by that company's owner) and migrations (two append-only tables and thirteen guard triggers). A separate agent read the first version of this slice (commit a644577) and found one high, three medium and six low findings; the fixes are migration 0007 and the code and tests after it ([VALIDATION.md](VALIDATION.md)). No person has read this slice.

## What works

- An owner presses "Make an assessment from version N" on `/diagnose`. The engine reads the newest version of the description they confirmed (never a later draft), ranks the opportunity library against it, and the result is stored. Making it again from the same version, library and engine returns the assessment already there (200, not 201), because the engine is deterministic. A newly confirmed version gives a new assessment; the old one is untouched.
- The engine is rules, not a model. The ranking is written out in `src/engine.ts` and on the page ("How the ideas were ranked"): 10 points for each problem the owner named that the idea addresses, plus points for readiness (4, 2 or 0), fit with the yearly budget (2, 0 or -2), minus risk (0, 1 or 2) and 1 for a missing answer about the gap. There is no term for AI. A test turns every item's kind around and checks that no score or position moves.
- Each idea carries: AI or not, why it is there (in the owner's own answers), benefit range with its measure, one-time, yearly and first-year cost in rupees, hidden costs, what to do first or check, risk and its note, and the source. Ideas whose gap the owner's answers show does not exist (they already keep accounts in software, say) are listed as left out, with the answer that excluded them. The first eight are shown.
- A result stores a copy of every library fact it used, so changing the library never changes a past assessment.
- The owner decides on each idea (accept, later, no), with a note. The latest decision on an idea stands; earlier ones stay in the history. The sign-off needs a decision on every idea, and after it nothing can be decided. It stores who, when, and a SHA-256 fingerprint of the assessment's id and result and the decision standing on each idea, with its note. Every read recomputes the fingerprint and says whether it still matches. The fingerprint does not cover who made a decision or when, the sign-off's own note, or the earlier decisions that a later one replaced (limit 3).
- The scope rule is the platform's own. Another company's owner, and the operator, get a 404 identical to a missing record, and the attempt is audited (`assessment.read.denied`, `assessment.write.denied`). A person in the company who is not its owner may read, and gets 403 on any change. An assessment id from another company, named under one's own company, is a 404. Every query is bound to the signed-in person's company; a static test checks every SQL string on both tables and that only the data layer touches them.
- The database, not only the application, holds the rules. Both tables refuse UPDATE, DELETE and any INSERT that would replace a row: an assessment on its id or on its inputs, an event on its id. A second sign-off for the same assessment is refused by a trigger of its own, so `INSERT OR REPLACE` cannot delete the first (the review's high finding; before migration 0007 it could). Further triggers refuse an assessment from a draft or missing version, a decision on an idea that is not in the assessment, a decision after sign-off, a sign-off with an undecided idea, a sign-off on an assessment with no ideas, and a 201st decision on one assessment (200 is an assumption). A composite foreign key stops an event from naming another company's assessment. The health check reports `degraded` if any guard is missing. A test in the platform reads every unique key of every append-only table from the migrated database and requires a BEFORE INSERT trigger that looks at each one, so a key added later without a matching trigger fails.
- Created decisions and sign-offs write an audit entry in the same batch, and only when the row was added. The sign-off statement carries the number of decisions its fingerprint was computed over; if another landed meanwhile it changes nothing and says "changed".
- A form post that cannot be done (an assessment that is not there, a decision that landed while the owner was signing off, a full assessment, an empty one) goes back to the page with a message, not to a page of JSON. Another company's assessment gets exactly the message a missing one gets. An exception: a form post refused *before* the person is known (no session 401, another company named in the path 404, the operator 404, a request another website started 403, a body over 64 KB 413) is answered with JSON, because the shared platform check `authorizeCompanyWrite` does not know it is a form; see modules/run/STATUS.md and its test, which pins each answer.
- The library, the ranking points and the engine's output for eight fixed descriptions are pinned by hash to LIBRARY_VERSION and ENGINE_VERSION. Changing any of them without a new version, or recording a version without its pin, fails a test.
- The page shows all of this in rupees with Indian digit grouping, says in the page what drafted it ("by a set of rules, not by an AI model"), and marks every figure SYNTHETIC.

## The page after the interface rebuild (slice 10a)

`/diagnose` ("What to do about it") was rebuilt on the shared interface of slice 10 (`app/globals.css`, `app/_ui/`, [the interface guide](../../docs/design/INTERFACE-GUIDE.md)). Behaviour did not change: the same forms post the same fields to the same endpoints, the redirects and `?problem=` keys are the same, and every message, label, button name, `data-testid` and notice role that the tests find the page by is the same. What changed is what a person sees.

- **The top of the page** shows the five steps (Decide underlined), one sentence on what the page is, the notices, and the SYNTHETIC line (the same words, now a quiet help line with a tag instead of a notice). Until an assessment exists for the newest confirmed description, "Make an assessment from version N" is the one main button, in a card that says what it will do; after that it is a quiet button in "Your assessments" (making it again gives the same assessment, as before). An owner with no confirmed description is shown why, and a button to Describe.
- **The assessment** opens with what it was made from, "Older version" when a newer description has been confirmed since, the line that says it was drafted by rules and not by a model, and the problems the owner named. Then comes one card above the ideas, with a bar of "N of M ideas decided.", one sentence on what is left, and a link to the next idea to decide. The sign-off button is the main button only when every idea is decided (it is disabled, with the meaning of signing off one tap away, until then). Once signed off the card is calm and green: when it was signed and the note, the fingerprint and whether it still matches, and "Next:" with the step the journey says is the owner's turn and a button in the journey's own words (for example "Make a request"), which is nothing when the owner's turn is still Describe or Decide.
- **Each idea is a card**: its rank and title (the heading keeps its text, "1. Keep every job ..."), tags for the owner's decision (or "Not decided yet"), AI or not, readiness and risk, the summary, one line "Answers:" with the owner's own problems, the facts (what it could do, with the SYNTHETIC wording; one-time cost; every year after; first year in all and whether it fits the budget), what to do first or check, the hidden costs, the risk, why it is here, and a collapsed "Source and score" (the source, and how the engine's points added up for this idea). The decision is three choices, a note and "Save decision on ..." beside the idea on a wide screen and under it on a phone; the save button of the first idea without a decision is the main button. After sign-off the choices go and the decision is one line.
- **Logic added** is in `src/progress.ts` and tested in `tests/unit/progress.test.ts`: the state of the sign-off (deciding, ready, signed, empty) and its sentence, the first undecided idea, whether an assessment is from an older description, the words for a decision, the line that shows how an idea's points were made up (the engine's own parts, which the test checks add up to the score for every idea of a real result), and where a signed-off assessment points next. Nothing in it reads another company's record.
- **No script runs on the page.** It is server-rendered with plain forms. It has its own small stylesheet (`app/diagnose/diagnose.css`, every class prefixed `dg-`).

## Not built

- The library is SYNTHETIC. Seventeen entries (13 without AI, 4 with) whose titles, conditions, figures and risks are placeholders. None is sourced; every figure is marked as an assumption. They exist to exercise the engine and must not be shown to a real owner as advice. Real content needs OQ-09 (the right to reuse the study's material, with disclosure), OQ-03 (the first sector) and a named custodian (OQ-04).
- No sector-specific content. The questionnaire's sector answer is carried in the result but nothing in the library or the ranking depends on it.
- No advisor or consultant. The thesis says "AI drafts; a person checks"; here the person who checks is the owner, because adding a second person who reads a company's record is a data-scope change that needs its own review and decisions about who that person is (OQ-08). Slice 3 deliberately does not widen the scope rule.
- No AI. Nothing calls a model. If a model ever drafts items or explanations, that is a new slice with its own evidence.
- Nothing about what happens after "accept". An accepted idea does not yet become a requirement; that is slice 4.
- Re-assessing after a change to a draft. Only confirmed versions are used, so a draft changes nothing until the owner confirms it.
- English only; no export; no print view; no comparison between two assessments.

## Known limits

1. The ranking weights (10, 4, 2, 2, -2, risk, -1) are choices, not findings. They produce a defensible order for the synthetic library; nothing shows they are the right trade-offs for a real owner.
2. Costs are compared with the top of the yearly budget band the questionnaire offers (round numbers, assumptions). The first year counts the one-time cost and one year of the recurring cost; benefit is not netted against cost, so there is no payback figure.
3. The owner signs off their own assessment. The sign-off is a record that they read and decided, not a check by anyone else. The fingerprint detects a change to what it covers (the assessment's result and the standing decisions with their notes) made outside the application; it does not cover who decided or when, the sign-off's own note, or earlier decisions that were replaced, so a change to those goes unseen. It does not stop a change either (SQLite cannot stop `DROP TRIGGER`), and nothing stores the fingerprint anywhere else. Covering the whole event log would change the digest, so it would be a new version of the digest and not an edit.
4. A decision note is free text up to 500 characters (in the table too after migration 0014 on the review-fixes branch, which also bounds it at 2,000 bytes because SQLite's `length()` stops at a NUL); the handler counts characters only and, unlike the factory module's, does not refuse control characters, so a note with a NUL in it is stored as text (found by the review of the review fixes); nothing reads it. A decision with no decision in it (NULL passed the shape check) is refused by the same migration.
5. Making an assessment is cheap and idempotent, but each new confirmed version adds a row that can never be removed. Once the review-fixes branch is applied, a company can save at most 500 versions of its description (an assumption), which bounds this; until then nothing limits how many versions an owner can confirm.
6. The guards test and the health check confirm that triggers exist by name. They do not run them; the integration tests prove behaviour with real SQL, one probe per rule, and a trigger with the right name and a weaker body would pass the name checks.
7. The page was run in Chromium only, against a local Worker. The inherited platform limits apply (1, 8, 9 and 11 in [the platform status](../platform/STATUS.md)).

## Where your factory stands, and what changed since the earlier assessment (slice 11a, 6 October 2026)

Two sections and one honesty change were added to `/diagnose` ("What to do about it"): a view of where the factory stands on data, people, systems, money and quality (BR-DIAG-08), a view of what changed since the earlier assessment (BR-DIAG-11), and a plain statement on each idea of where its figures come from and whether anything has to come first (BR-DIAG-02). The author is an agent (branch `slice-11a-diagnose-gap`); no person has read it and no owner has used it. No endpoint, table, migration or package was added, and the engine, the library, the API and the data layer were not touched. Both new views are pure code in `src/gaps.ts` and `src/compare.ts` that read stored results; the page reads them with the company-bound `loadAssessment` and `loadRevision` calls it already used.

### What works

- **Where your factory stands** sits below the ideas, with a link to it under the assessment's heading. It has five parts in the order the requirements name them: Data, People, Systems, Money, Quality. Each part shows what it is about, the owner's own answers it reads (folded, one tap), and "What stands in the way": what has to come first, what is to be checked because an answer is missing, an answer missing to see whether the idea applies at all, and the idea's first-year cost against the top of the yearly budget band. Each line names the idea and its place. Ideas left out because of an answer in that part are listed with the answer. A part with nothing in the way says "Nothing in the way". The same lines can be read idea by idea (folded). `gapView` returns the lines both ways and a test requires that they are the same lines.
- **Every line is something the engine already judged**, in the library's own words, put in the part of the question it is about. No rule, threshold, rating, score or figure was added. The money line quotes the idea's stored first-year range and the band's ceiling, both SYNTHETIC assumptions.
- **Which part a question belongs to is a choice for this page, not a finding.** The page says so, and `AREA_OF_QUESTION` in `src/gaps.ts` is the one place it is written. A test requires that every question the rules read is in a part and that every question in a part is read by the rules.
- **"Not assessed" where the library cannot support a part.** The library reads one question about people (who looks after computers and software), so People shows that answer and says skills and staffing are not assessed. Money is not assessed when no yearly budget band was given or the owner said they are not sure. Data, People, Systems and Quality are not assessed when none of their answers was given, and the part names the question. If the description an assessment was made from cannot be read, every part says so and the lines the stored result holds are still shown.
- **A library version guard.** Lines are placed in a part with the current library only when the assessment was made with that library version. Otherwise they are kept and shown as "not placed in a part", with the reason.
- **What changed since the earlier assessment** compares the assessment shown with the one made just before it (the next older one in "Your assessments"), above the ideas. It shows which version of the description each is from; "Your answers that changed" (answers the rules read first; a free-text answer the rules do not read shows only that it changed; the order a many-choice answer was saved in is not a change); ideas that moved up or down, with the places and the reason taken from the stored points (problems answered, readiness, fit with the budget, a missing answer, risk, or "Its own points did not change. Other ideas moved around it."), and any prerequisite that changed, naming the changed answer only when it is among the answers that changed; ideas that came in or dropped out, with the engine's own reason for a left-out idea or "did not match a problem you named"; ideas that kept their place but changed in what they need; and how many stayed the same. When both were made from the same description, it says that any difference comes from the library or the ranking rules being a different version. It changes nothing and stores nothing.
- **Each idea says where its figures come from**: "an assumption (SYNTHETIC placeholder). No study, quotation or measurement stands behind them", or the source register entry when the library names one (none does today). An idea with nothing to do or check first says "Nothing the library asks for first is missing." The browser test reads each idea's source from the library, so the page cannot name a source the library does not.
- **Empty states**: no assessment yet (what the two sections will show and what makes them appear, in different words when no description is confirmed); one assessment ("nothing to compare it with"); the earliest of several (nothing before it, with a link to the newest); every idea left out (said in the ideas area and in the part view, nothing to sign off); no idea matches the problems named (the parts still show the answers).
- **Phone**: at 390 pixels there is no sideways scroll and every link, fold and button in the page body is at least 44 pixels tall (three links that were 20 pixels tall, one of them new, were fixed). The browser test checks this with and without the comparison open.
- **Another company's owner** who names the address of an assessment is shown none of it: no comparison, no answers, no ideas (browser test).

### Not built

- No level, rating or score for a part, and no comparison with other factories or a sector. The library has nothing to base one on.
- Nothing about people beyond who looks after the software: skills, staffing and training are not assessed because no rule reads them.
- Only the assessment made just before can be compared; the owner cannot pick another. The comparison is a list of what changed, not a side-by-side layout.
- The engine's own sentences are shown as it wrote them: the reason for a left-out idea ends "so the gap does not seem to exist here", in the comparison and in the existing list of left-out ideas. The word is not in the new text (a unit test checks the gap view's words), but changing the engine's sentences needs a new engine version and new pins.
- Not shown to an owner. The wording of the two sections, the jump line and the empty states has not been checked in a ten-owner test.
- "No comparison between two assessments", in the list above, is superseded by this section.

### Limits of this slice

1. The page is long on a phone: about 11,000 pixels tall at 390 pixels wide for a second assessment with every fold closed. The ideas come first and the two sections are reached by links, but a short page would need a decision about what to fold or move.
2. The five parts are only as rich as the library. With seventeen SYNTHETIC entries, People has one question behind it and Quality has one. A real library may need different parts or different questions in them.
3. Every figure the page can show is an assumption. The statement about sources is true because no library entry cites one; it must be checked again when one does.
4. Chromium only, a local Worker, the synthetic sign-in. No real phone, no screen reader. A keyboard walk was done once by script (see [VALIDATION.md](VALIDATION.md)).
